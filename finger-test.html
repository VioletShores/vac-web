Per-finger detection (live)

Straight-angle threshold: 35° lower = stricter
What we're testing: each finger is judged by the angle between its base→middle and middle→tip segments — a straight finger keeps those segments roughly aligned (small angle) regardless of which way the hand points; a curled finger bends them sharply. That's why it should survive rotation. Known hard case (flagged in the S101 review): a finger pointing straight at the lens foreshortens in 2D and may misread — test that one deliberately so we see the real limit. The thumb uses a separate spread test. Nothing here touches the real auth yet.
