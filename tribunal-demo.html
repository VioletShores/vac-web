End-to-end architecture

1
Verified parties
Each party authenticates via VAC identity — face liveness, voiceprint, gesture, OTP, weighted to a trust score. A verified human becomes the root of an authority chain.
Live · Gemini liveness + Deepgram voiceprint + multi-factor
→
2
Policy-carrying packet
The trust deed's rules — release conditions, beneficiaries, trigger events — are encoded into the matter itself. The policy travels with the work, not on a central server.
VAC packet · rules sealed to the matter
3
Condition evaluation
A trigger event (settlement notice, death registration, evidence submission) is verified and checked against the packet's predicates. Simple cases satisfy automatically; conflicts are routed out.
Deterministic policy evaluation
→
4
Human authorises
A person — conveyancer, executor, tribunal officer — reviews the complete, condition-checked matter and authorises the outcome. The protocol never decides; it prepares.
Human-in-the-loop · always
5
Sealed & lodged decision — real cryptography
The authorised decision is signed with an Ed25519 key as a compact JWT, given a unique token id, and lodged to the authority chain. Anyone can verify it against VAC's public key — tamper-evident, timestamped, independently checkable. This is live technology, not a mock.
Live · Ed25519 + JWT · verifiable on vacprotocol.org · 285 patent claims

The same protocol, other high-stakes domains

Verified human authority, policy that travels with the work, and a sealed, auditable decision trail are not specific to legal trusts. The same primitives apply wherever who authorised what, under which rules has to be provable after the fact.

Defence & sovereign systems
Chain-of-command authority for autonomous and AI-assisted systems — a verifiable record that a cleared human authorised an action, under encoded rules of engagement, that an oversight body can audit. Sovereignty-sensitive by design: policy stays with the operator, not on a vendor's servers.
Regulated finance & custody
Authorisation trails for high-value transactions where an auditor must later prove a verified human signed off under specific conditions — without exposing the underlying confidential rules.
Critical infrastructure & energy
Verifiable authority for actions on physical systems — who is permitted to act, under what conditions, with a tamper-evident record for the regulator.
