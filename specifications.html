Specifications

The drafts behind the protocol

The protocol is written up as individual Internet-Drafts at the IETF.

Status: individual drafts, not standards. An Internet-Draft is a work-in-progress document that anyone may post. These have not been adopted by an IETF working group and carry no endorsement from the IETF.
Posted drafts

Verified Human Root Attestation for Agent Delegation Chains and Audit Records

draft-zagarella-verified-human-root

Defines a compact, privacy-preserving attestation that a verified natural person, or an M-of-N group of people, stands at the head of a delegation chain. The binding survives each narrowing hop, and a verifier can resolve any recorded action to an accountable person without receiving biometric material.

Intended status: Informational. Offered as input to the proposed AUDIT working group’s data-model work.

Datatracker Read -01 (text)
Pre-action assurance · current revision -01 · expires …

Pre-Action Risk-Graded Assurance for Agent Interactions

draft-zagarella-autonomy-governor

Describes a stage that runs before an agent acts. It derives how much assurance the action needs from a risk signal, states that in a form suited to the action, and records the decision in an audit record that can be bound to a verified human root.

Intended status: Informational. Designed to compose with the verified-human-root draft.

How they fit

Who stood at the root, and how much latitude they gave

The first draft answers who authorised an action. The second records how much assurance the action needed before it ran.

Together they let a reviewer, years later, establish both the accountable person at the origin of an agent action and the assurance that was required and met at the moment it happened. Neither draft standardises biometric methods or risk-scoring methods; they standardise the records and their bindings.

Revision history
