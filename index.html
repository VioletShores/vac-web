VAC Protocol · Verified Authority Chain

Every agent action, traced back to a verified person.

VAC carries a person’s verified authority through chains of AI agents. The person verifies once. A signed token records who they are, what they allowed and for how long. Each delegation can only narrow it, and anyone can check the chain without seeing any biometric data.

One chain

From the person, all the way down

Agents can act for a person. The chain proves which person, under what limits, at the moment of the action.

A Verified Authority Token (VAT) is signed at the root. Every hop derives a narrower token. A verifier at the action checks every hop back to the person, and the check fails closed if any hop is missing, expired or revoked.

What the chain proves

Which verified person stands at the root, the scope they granted, the assurance level of each hop, and that nothing downstream exceeded it.

How it works

What a verifier never sees

Checking a chain needs no biometric data. A verifier receives a signed attestation and a hashed reference to the person, never a face, a voice or a template.

Where it stands

Two individual Internet-Drafts are posted at the IETF, with a third in preparation. A reference service is running, and the inventions are patent pending.
