The gap

As AI becomes agentic, liability follows authorisation — yet no infrastructure links an audited machine decision back to a live, verified natural person with enforceable bounds. Courts already treat this as the question: in Australia, Pintarich v DCT [2018] FCAFC 79 held a computer-generated output is not a valid decision without a human's mental process bound to it; the US agent-access cases now put a clock on the same gap.

Live now — the verified human root (VAC Protocol)

A verified human root: multi-modal biometric presence (face embedding, cross-modal spoken-digit binding, passive liveness) seals a decision as an Ed25519-signed, independently verifiable credential. Every AI agent's authority chains to that root, stays bounded by it, and traces back to it. Proportional re-authentication matches ceremony weight to stakes.

The second instrument — ground-truth harnesses for model selection

Athena runs a known-answer challenge harness across frontier and open models, scoring each per domain and maintaining a calibrated trust graph — so orchestration routes each task to the model the evidence says is best, with provenance for every answer. For computational science this is the reproducibility question in modern dress: which method, for which problem, calibrated against ground truth — the discipline simulation science has always demanded, applied to LLMs.

Research surface A

Trust calibration methodology: per-domain known-answer testing, decay, and cross-model adversarial critique as a publishable evaluation framework.

Research surface B

Accountable agentic pipelines for science: simulation and ML workflows whose every consequential step is sealed under a bounded human authority — auditable computational provenance.
