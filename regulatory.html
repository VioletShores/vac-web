Regulatory

Built to evidence human oversight

Regulation increasingly asks organisations to prove that an authorised person oversaw an AI system’s consequential actions. VAC is designed to produce that evidence.

Alignment, not certification. The maps below show where VAC is designed to support an obligation. Whether a deployment meets an obligation is determined with your assessor; VAC is not certified against any of these frameworks.
EU AI Act

Where VAC supports the Act’s obligations

ObligationArticlesHow VAC is designed to support it
Human oversight14A verified, present person authorises the consequential action, producing a record that the oversight happened.
Record-keeping and logs12, 19Every authorisation emits a signed action record that a third party can verify without trusting the operator.
Deployer obligations26Each agent’s authority is scoped under a named, verified person, so responsibility has a checkable answer.
Transparency13, 50Whether an action traces to a person directly or to an agent acting under one is visible in the chain.

On record with NIST

Liveness component

VAC uses a third-party passive-liveness detector that has been independently tested against ISO/IEC 30107-3 presentation attacks. VAC’s own engine is not certified.
