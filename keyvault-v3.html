Step 3 — Vault PIN
Key derivation
Your wrapping key is derived using PBKDF2 (250,000 iterations, SHA-256) from your vault PIN combined with your ceremony-verified email. This computation happens entirely in your browser via the Web Crypto API. The PIN is never stored. The operator sees only ciphertext + salt — and cannot derive your key without your PIN.

What was done
Your seed was encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from: your PIN + your verified email + a random vault salt. The plaintext seed is gone from memory. Only the ciphertext, IV, and salt are stored. Your vault PIN was never saved.
Production seam: In production, ciphertext would be pushed to POST /v1/vault/seed/store after presenting a valid L3 VAT JTI. That endpoint does not yet exist in vac-protocol — the demo stores in localStorage. The retrieval endpoint GET /v1/vault/seed/{vault_id} would require a fresh L3 JTI before returning the ciphertext.

Roadmap Not Live

Features below are designed but not yet built. They are clearly labeled — none of the following is live functionality.

Recovery Guardians ROADMAP
Design intent (F-1073)
M-of-N named guardians each complete their own independent VAC L3 ceremony before the seed is released. 2-of-2 spousal ceremony is the consumer wedge: your Bitcoin cannot leave without both of you, alive, on camera, sealed. Each guardian sighting is its own sealed receipt; the release seal composes all of them.
Guardian 1 ROADMAP
Primary holder — you
Guardian 2 ROADMAP
Named family member or co-signer — must independently complete L3 ceremony
M-of-N quorum ROADMAP
Configurable threshold. Level changes require a ceremony at the current level. Class floors (F-1074) are non-negotiable.
Duress Detection ROADMAP
Design intent (F-1074)
A duress biometric signal (detected from video/voice analysis during the ceremony) triggers a silent alarm — access is denied or decoy material is returned, without alerting the threat. The full ceremony + cooldown + notification-to-all-parties path is the CRITICAL class hard floor; it cannot be configured below this level for seed phrases.
Silent alarm on duress signal ROADMAP
Ceremony detects involuntary stress markers; access denied silently
Decoy output mode ROADMAP
Under duress, a decoy seed is returned — attacker sees something; real seed is never exposed
Server-Gated Ciphertext ROADMAP
Production seam (F-538)
The current demo stores ciphertext in localStorage. Production requires a server-side vault endpoint that returns ciphertext ONLY after verifying a fresh L3 VAT JTI. This adds a second trust layer: even if the device is compromised, the ciphertext is not locally present without a ceremony. Endpoints designed:
POST /v1/vault/seed/store ROADMAP
Accepts: vat_jti (L3), vault_salt, iv, ciphertext. Returns vault_id. No plaintext ever reaches the server.
GET /v1/vault/seed/{vault_id} ROADMAP
Returns ciphertext ONLY after fresh L3 JTI verification. Sealed retrieval receipt issued per access.
