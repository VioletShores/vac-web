Where VAC connects

The same authority, used three ways

Decisions carry a VAC receipt

An Athena Decisions record states who decided and with what authority, in a receipt anyone can check without access to Athena.

Vault waits for a verified grant

Athena Vault releases a credential only after the owner completes a VAC verification, using the cross-site handoff described on the developer page.

L0 packets name their authority

Every governed work packet records the verified person whose authority it runs under, so the evidence trail starts at a person, not an account.
