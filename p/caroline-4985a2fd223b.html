A supplementary filing candidate is drafted and waiting on your eyes before Rob files the provisional. The groups, in plain terms:

  1. Sealed adaptation explanations — when verification requirements adapt to trust, the explanation shown to the person is derived only from the real decision inputs and sealed into the receipt. The explanation is itself evidence.

  5. Attested-history evaluation harnesses — candidate capabilities are tested against challenges generated from the system's own sealed execution records, so the ground truth of every test is cryptographically verifiable. We believe evidence-grade benchmarks are a new category.
