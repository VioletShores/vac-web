Coding SignalRank — the API we planned to ship

Where the test harness actually is research complete · runs not yet fired

The research phase finished 18 Jul with zero spend: benchmark landscape mapped (SWE-bench Verified, LiveCodeBench, Aider-polyglot, BigCodeBench all reproducible by us), a five-task-class coding corpus designed to the existing challenge-harness schema with execution-verified scoring (code runs, output checked — no judge opinion needed for most of it), and a cost model. The build itself was awaiting a go — no calibration has run yet, which is why no coding scores exist.

Kimi K3 reality check from the research: of Moonshot\u2019s published numbers, only SWE-bench Verified (65.8%) and LiveCodeBench (67.2%) are independently reproducible; Terminal-Bench is partially; SWE-Marathon and ProgramBench are internal marketing numbers and stay OUT of our trust graph until we run them ourselves.
