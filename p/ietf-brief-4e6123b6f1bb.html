CHEQ vs your two drafts — the distinction that matters

CHEQ (Rosenberg/Jennings) confirms a specific action before it happens — a Confirmation Server shows the user the pending action, they approve, it signs a "CHEQ object". Per-action, real-time, in MCP/A2A. Stops LLM hallucinations causing unwanted actions.
Your AUTONOMY-GOVERNOR = risk-graded policy: decides WHICH actions need what level of assurance. CHEQ is the mechanism; governor is the policy that decides when it fires. ⚠ Overlaps CHEQ on "confirming actions" — DON'T position as competing. Frame as the grading layer ABOVE CHEQ.
Your VERIFIED-HUMAN-ROOT — your clean, uncontested ground CHEQ §4.2 says, in its own words: "CHEQ does not attempt to verify that the user is actually a human being." It confirms an authenticated ACCOUNT approved something — it explicitly does NOT verify a real human or root the chain in one. That hole is your peg. Verified-human-root + authority lineage fills the exact slot CHEQ disclaims. Cite their own draft as evidence the gap exists.
THE THREAD-IT LINE: "CHEQ confirms that an authenticated account approved an action — but it explicitly doesn't verify a real human is there, or root the chain in one. That human-root and its authority lineage is the layer I've been working on. It composes with CHEQ, it doesn't replace it."
Patent position — checked against your FILED claims (not concepts) Verified-human-root = Claims 168–178 (Supp #3, provenance/sovereignty): monotonic append-only provenance chain, NIST 800-63 assurance levels, "proof a specific human was biometrically verified at a specific assurance level before any agent action." CHEQ has NO provenance chain and NO human-root — different axis entirely. Clean.

Autonomy-governor = Action-Hash family (Claims 369/402, grading 378/411). Independent claim = compute an Action Hash (action + timestamp + delegating human's identity) → derive a BIOMETRIC challenge from it → verify against enrolled biometric. CHEQ explicitly disclaims human verification (§4.2) and has no biometrics, no action-derived challenge. Your claims live in the exact dimension CHEQ avoids. Risk-grading (low/med/high/critical → session/face/multimodal/vouch) is uncontested — CHEQ is binary confirm-reject. Differentiated at the element level.
