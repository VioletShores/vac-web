The two alerts that matter most

VAC occupies two gaps in the entire agent-identity landscape. The board exists to catch either one closing:

  • Nobody roots agent delegation in a verified human biometric. Alert the moment anyone adds a strong biometric human root to agent delegation.
  • No draft or product unifies holder-attenuable delegation with provenance. Alert if one appears.

IETF frontier moving monthly

  • CHEQ (Rosenberg) — human-in-the-loop confirmation of agent decisions. Closest to our territory; complementary (runtime confirmation, no verified-person root). Composition posture holds.
  • AIMS — the leading agent-auth framework (CyberArk/Zscaler/AWS/Ping + now OpenAI/Okta authors, v-02 Jun). Watch for working-group adoption.
  • AUDIT working-group proposal (Kühlewind/Birkholz) — agent audit records + delegation traceability. Our natural home; charter progress is the single most consequential line on this board. List post lands Wed 29.
  • PTV — hardware-anchored attested agent identity naming an HDP+SCITT stack; "Agent Identity Protocol" draft collides with our AIP acronym — both tracked for prior-art positioning.
  • Nelson delegation-receipts at version -10 with an MIT SDK — fast-moving solo author; assumes the human root is solved (hardware key, not biometric). Real prior-art class, minor market threat; alert if biometrics appear.

Commercial contest

  • Incode "Agentic Identity" watch closely — the real commercial contest: binds agents to owner biometrics + token + behavioural monitoring, ahead on go-to-market. Does not do fused-modality liveness, spatial co-location, or a caveat-chain root — the gap we hold.
  • Idira (Palo Alto/CyberArk) — agentic privileged-access control plane. No biometric root; better read as a channel candidate than a competitor, unless it moves down-stack.
  • Agent-auth platforms (Aembit, Stytch, WorkOS, Arcade et al) and liveness incumbents (iProov, FaceTec, HYPR et al) — watched for either side closing toward the middle we occupy.

Bodies & anchors

  • NIST 800-63-4 final; watching for conformance-criteria release — directly relevant to our conformance-test play.
  • GNAP (our extension target) — core and resource-server RFCs final, working group quiet; watching for revival.
  • Embodied AI — no one yet roots robot authority in a verified human; if Figure/1X/Nvidia-class players or the robotics-safety bodies move there, it overlaps our whole moat in the physical domain.
