Budget rails

One rail family, three layers: P0.8 sets the aggregate cap across all spend, F-133 is the per-provider/per-key spend rail underneath it, and F-927 caps individual turn budgets so no single call blows past its share. Together they form the COPS/PID cap plan — aggregate ceiling, provider rails, per-turn floor, all enforced from the same policy source.

How this fills

A Gmail connector watches for spend, auto-reload, and receipt emails from each provider, and provider usage APIs are polled directly where available. Both sources land in the same place and populate the provider rows above — until that connector is attached, the rows stay honest placeholders.
