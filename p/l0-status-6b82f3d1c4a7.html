The gaps — in build order

1 · North Star envelope into the packet core step 1 building today

The header that lets a packet run unattended and stay accountable: who authorised it, under which policy, with what budget, and its compressed link into the constellation's shared awareness. Step 1 (structure + tests) is in a lane right now; wiring and the runner follow. This is the single gate for sentinel firing, the dreams cycle, and the sentinel page — three features converged on it today.

2 · Repo capability decision pending — yours

Sentinels can't yet write to the repo, so they can't maintain the boards and watch entries themselves. Design is done: a tightly scoped credential, append-only paths, distinct sentinel identity, CI-enforced. Open decision for you: credential held directly by the product (travel-resilient, works when the Mini is off — recommended) versus routing repo writes through the Mini (couples the dailies to Mini uptime).

3 · Sentinel runner not built

The scheduler graduates from firing bare jobs to executing full packets — plan, execute, verify, seal, remember — with every run linked into the constellation. The dreams cycle's five job types (stitch, compress, triage, scan, brief) all execute here.

4 · Migrate the dailies after runner

Heartbeat v2, the nightly standards/market scan (finally honest — no chat ritual), the routing-integrity audit, and the strategic review each become a sentinel packet with a receipt.

5 · Command surface last

You reply on WhatsApp — approve, re-prioritise, dispatch, "brief me on X" — and the product parses and acts. Authority rule stands: sentinels recommend; only your reply or a policy rail changes priorities.

The gate — when daily ops moves to AthenaPilot

Measurable, no vibes: seven consecutive days where every brief you receive was produced by sentinel packets with honest stamps, plus at least one successful WhatsApp command round-trip per day, plus the Engine Room green on sentinel commit discipline. Then chat becomes deep-work only.
