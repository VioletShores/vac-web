What the VAC family covers

In plain words

Verified human authority

Multi-modal biometric verification of the person at the root, with graduated trust levels and continuous trust scoring.

Delegation chains

Tokens that carry human attribution through agent delegation, narrowing at each hop, inside and across organisations.

Revocation and validity

Cascading revocation, and authority treated as a continuously checked state rather than a one-off grant.

Provenance records

Tamper-evident records of authorised actions with jurisdictional and verification context at each hop.

Collective authority

Multi-party and community-scoped approval, including support for Indigenous data-sovereignty principles.

Physical systems

Verified human authority over drones, robots and industrial systems, with physical limits expressed as scope.

The Verified Authority Chain: verified human authority, carried through agent delegation, checkable by anyone.
