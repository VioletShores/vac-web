Decision — PI reviews the multi-model analysis and accepts GPE-4C

Human decision · identity not yet verified · authority not yet sealed

The Principal Investigator reviews the MD-ranked shortlist and the three specialist model contributions — cross-checking the structural analysis against the toxicity profile, the literature synthesis against the institutional research protocol, and the MD trajectories against the study's predefined selection criteria.

The PI accepts GPE-4C (Generation-4 PEG C-terminus dendrimer) as the primary candidate for wet-lab follow-up. At this point a scientific decision has been made — but no authority has been verified and no record has been sealed. If the decision were logged only in the study management system, the audit trail would rest on that system's integrity. VAC replaces that assumption with a cryptographic proof: not just who clicked accept, but who held institutional authority to accept this class of recommendation, under what conditions, at what time.

Seal — VAC verifies the PI and seals the acceptance

Ed25519 signed token · live production backend · independently verifiable
