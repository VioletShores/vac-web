# Market Wedge — Accountability via the EU AI Act Retrofit

## The resonant word: accountability

Agentic AI is crossing into consequential actions — payments, filings, releases, infrastructure changes. Every one of those actions needs an answer to *who authorised this?* that stands up afterwards: to an auditor, a regulator, or a court.

The incumbent answer is screenshots and log-scraping. That is not accountability; it is archaeology.

## The wedge: EU AI Act retrofit

The EU AI Act's enforcement wave turns this from good practice into a budgeted, board-level compliance obligation across every regulated organisation in Europe. Article 50 deepfake-disclosure obligations took effect 2 August 2026. The tranche applying to high-risk AI system operators is live. Organisations that have already deployed AI must make those deployments accountable — not build new ones.

This is the wedge: the zero-integration retrofit. A seal-request link and an evidence page. No code changes on the customer's side. An existing AI deployment becomes auditable in one afternoon.

## The gap no incumbent owns

Identity vendors verify humans at login — not per action. Observability vendors record what happened — not who was accountable for it. The gap between those two is exactly where VAC sits: per-action, verified-human authority, with a tamper-evident receipt — and a 1,068-claim patent estate and an open standards track staked around it.

No incumbent owns this position. The EU AI Act has created a funded compliance obligation to fill it.
