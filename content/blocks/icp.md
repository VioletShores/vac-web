# Ideal Customer Profile (F-1056)

**Accountable-decision sectors first** — legal/tribunal (evidence that survives an auditor), water and critical infrastructure (operational decisions with regulatory weight), and defence-adjacent — reached through the EU AI Act retrofit wedge: organisations that must make *existing* AI deployments accountable, not build new ones.

The entry is always one irreversible action class: the decision that cannot be undone, that must be auditable, and that currently has no trail beyond a screenshot. Once the first action class is sealed, the authority chain extends naturally across the organisation.

Design-partner sequence:
1. **Legal / tribunal** — the decision to release, settle, or advise; the evidence chain that survives discovery (Caroline, design-partner track active)
2. **Water / critical infrastructure** — the operational decision with regulatory weight; the control-system action that must be attributable (Antonio / AQP)
3. **Defence-adjacent** — the authorisation chain behind a consequential AI recommendation

University relationships (University of Catania, IDSIA/Danani) serve as credibility anchors and grant co-applicants — instrumental to the funding stack, not the product.
