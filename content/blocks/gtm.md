# Go-to-Market Sequence

## Near term: design-partner pilots on one irreversible action class
The entry offer is the zero-integration compliance retrofit: a seal-request link and an evidence page. No code changes on the customer's side. An existing AI deployment becomes auditable. Paid pilots in financial services and regulated-advice firms first.

The sequence matches the EU AI Act compliance urgency: organisations facing the highest enforcement risk are also the ones with the most acute need for an audit trail on AI-assisted decisions.

## In parallel: standards + IP moat
Two Internet-Drafts advancing at the IETF. The standards track is the moat narrative: by the time a major identity vendor acquires into this space, VAC is the referenced open standard — and the reference implementation is protected by the patent estate (1,068 claims, 17 filings).

## Funding stack
- **Italian SRL (Sicily)** — Smart&Start Italia: 30% grant + 70% interest-free loan, €100K–1.5M; rolling application window
- **Eurostars / Innosuisse** — joint research work-package with IDSIA/Danani; Sep 2026 window; each country funds its own side
- **EIC Accelerator 2027** — once design partners are signed and IDSIA validation is on record; up to €2.5M grant + €0.5–10M EIC Fund equity

Universities (University of Catania, IDSIA) serve as credibility anchors and Eurostars co-applicants — the academic relationship de-risks the grant application without requiring an academic product.

## Layered end-state
Open standard (anyone can implement) + protected reference implementation (VAC) + regional implementation partners + vendor-neutral advisory practice. When a board asks "who do we buy from?", the answer is clean at every layer.
