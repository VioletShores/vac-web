Your story — five stones, your own words

1 · The Stanford question (2000)
"At Stanford's exec program in 2000 I kept thinking: why should I be the privileged one who gets to tell my elevator story to a room like that? Everyone deserves that shot — but video and speech tech wasn't ready. I carried the idea for twenty-five years."
2 · Soul — FolioAI
"When agentic AI and video models arrived, the time was right — FolioAI: helping a person create their pitch video with soul. I tested every video model to find which could do it, met my co-founder Shan, and built the model around a deep domain expert's context — hers is cultural, indigenous, and it changed what the models produced."
The first expert-in-the-loop was a cultural guardian. Plant it — it returns as the harness ask.
3 · The gaps — SignalRank, then L0
"Testing models to pick the best for each job became an instrument — SignalRank, ground-truth calibration per domain. But abstracting above any single model exposed the deeper gaps: memory, context, state preservation. So I built the control plane: plan, research, execute, test, validate against a contract — and the research phase discovers new intelligence, then builds the right deep-domain harness to test ground truth against it. That's the L0 protocol."
4 · The irony — soul to trust
"Here's the part I didn't plan. I was building video analysis to bring out the soul of a human — and realised the AI systems acting everywhere had no verifiable human behind them at all. Same engine, deeper question. So the biometric multimodal work became about trust: audit, governance, authority — what a person is actually entitled to do."
